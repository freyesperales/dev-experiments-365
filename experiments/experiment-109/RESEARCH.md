# Research — Experiment 109 (Wednesday lane: web hygiene)

Run date: 2026-11-25 (UTC). Three scouts researched in parallel: what is rising this week, real defender pain, and what already exists. Then the lead agent downloaded every source the tool depends on and read the relevant passage in each. "Lead" means the lead agent fetched it (HTTP 200) during this run and read the quoted fact in the downloaded page. "Scout" means one scout opened it and the lead did not repeat the fetch. Sources that nobody could open are listed at the end and are not used.

## Judge

Scores 1–5 for usefulness to a small business × freshness × feasibility in one run × shareability.

| Candidate | Use | Fresh | Feasible | Share | Total | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Paste your page: outside code, mixed content, http:// links, SRI, and a report-only CSP built from it, checked against your current CSP** | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | **400** | Real pain (#21–#27), the only builder for owners is abandoned (#29), the evaluator does not build (#28). Chrome 154 (#6–#8), OPTIMJACK (#18) and Convertr (#19) make it timely. Vendor origins come from vendor docs. |
| Chrome 154 readiness checker on its own (domain + http links) | 4 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 240 | A browser cannot follow other sites' redirect chains (CORS); the part that works on pasted HTML is folded into the winner. |
| Dangling CNAME / subdomain takeover check over DNS-over-HTTPS | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 108 | Real gap, but the scouts found no owner describing it as their own problem; existing tools target hunters. Kept for a later Wednesday. |
| Beg-bounty email triage | 4 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 96 | Good idea, but mostly email-authentication findings (the Monday lane). |

Picked: **Aduana**. It overlaps Headerfix (096) only in the CSP grade; Headerfix grades headers you paste, while Aduana reads the page and writes the policy. It overlaps Trastienda (108) only in inventorying scripts; Trastienda is Shopify-only and about app owners.
