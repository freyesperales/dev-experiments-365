Pursuant to Section 4 of the Housing Benefit Regulations, the claimant is required to notify the Council in the event that any change in circumstances occurs, and failure to do so may result in the overpayment of benefit being recovered, together with a penalty (the amount of which will be determined at the discretion of the Council) in accordance with the relevant legislation. Prior to the commencement of the assessment, additional documentation must be submitted. We will endeavour to process your application as soon as possible.
