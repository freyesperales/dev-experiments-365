FeedFrock

Chrome is removing XSLT. Paste or drop your RSS, Atom or sitemap XML and get a static, JavaScript-free page that browsers can still show — plus the server snippet to serve it. Everything runs in this tab; nothing is uploaded.

Drop a feed / sitemap file here

Browsers send Accept: text/html; feed readers don't. These snippets serve feed.html to browsers and the untouched XML to everyone else. Test with curl -sI -H 'Accept: text/html' … on your host.
