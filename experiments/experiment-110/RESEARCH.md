# Research — Experiment 110 (Thursday lane: AI agents)

Run date: 2026-11-26 (UTC). Three scouts researched in parallel: what is rising this week, real defender pain, and what already exists. Then the lead agent downloaded every source the tool's rules depend on (HTTP 200 during this run) and read the passage quoted below. "Lead" means the lead agent fetched it and read the fact in the downloaded page. "Scout" means only a scout opened it. Sources nobody could open are listed at the end and are not used.
